Floor design review: inputs and scalability

Dragon Hood / terracart - what every floor shares, what differs, the complete inputs checklist for defining a floor declaratively, and the scalability ledger. Evidence: file:line from src/, verified Oct 2026.
One sentence: every underground floor derives from the tile above (recursing to the real OSM surface), so no floor is hand-drawn; identity comes from a per-floor terrain rule plus depth-keyed content tables. Content already scales in tables; the terrain rule, its look and the floor-locked feature flags still live as branches and literals - the FLOOR_PROFILES row is the agreed fix, and the descent gates now demonstrate the pattern.

1. The pipeline: how a floor gets made

A cave tile is generated on demand by loadCaveTile (worldgen.js:8768). It never loads stored map data; it derives everything from the level above, which derives from the surface. Core rule (worldgen.js:7914): a walkable cell above becomes CAVE_FLOOR, everything else CAVE_WALL, recursively up to depth 0.

  1. Recursion up: the level above's generated layer only (geologyGrid/genObjects), never the live entry with player edits (worldgen.js:8780). Depth ≥ 2 also re-reads the surface for immutable evidence.
  2. Terrain: undergroundTerrain(surface, aboveGrid, depth) (worldgen.js:8703) - the per-depth branch table in section 3.
  3. Spawn gate: floorSpawnWhy (worldgen.js:618) carries only the inherited hard reasons (SPAWN_WHY_ALL_FLOORS = FARMLAND|GOLF) from the frozen surface snapshot (caveSource, worldgen.js:6146).
  4. Stairs: down-stairs above become up-stairs at the same world point; wandering down-stairs seed from cellHash ^ depth (worldgen.js:8810-8854). L1 breaks the stair chain by design (rope/elevator descends); depth ≥ 2 gets a fallback shaft.
  5. Landing pockets: 3x3 floor around stairs and (depth 2) fall-sites (worldgen.js:8856).
  6. Geology freeze: geologyGrid = grid.slice() before the street-mirror overlay (floors ≤ 2), so carved streets never propagate down (worldgen.js:8871).
  7. Chest mirrors: POI chests mirror at the same GPS point, rank rides down (worldgen.js:8195); each level re-seats its own tier pyramid and prunes to 50 (worldgen.js:8232).
  8. Torches, quarry gems, authored areas, floor passes: torch sites mirror the floor above's low-tier POIs (worldgen.js:8269); spring caves claim grove anchors (floor 1); the CAVE_PASSES table (worldgen.js:8445) scatters the rest.
  9. Descent gates (new, tranche 1): the arena key gates entry to floor 5 (canEnterDepth); the portal stone rides a tier-4-or-better chest on floor 4 (≥ 1 km out); one table, DungeonProgression.ROPE_SEALED_FLOORS = [2,3,4,6], owns the rope refusal, the sinkhole minting refusal and the fall refusal - nothing descends past the elevator, dig or key segments by rope or by accident.
  10. Freeze for the next level: baseGrid/genObjects copies, so player edits never reroll the floor below.

5. Scalability ledger

Addressed

  • Descent rules are one table. The rope seal, the sinkhole-minting seal and the fall seal all read ROPE_SEALED_FLOORS; the arena gate and the portal stone moved to floors 4/5 with tests. A new gate is a row, not a scattered check.
  • The portal-stone bar survived the move. Floor 4's depth bonus lifts every chest to effective T3, so the bar was retuned to T4 - intent (a good chest, far out) kept, drift caught by a test.
  • Content was never the problem. Roster windows, THEME_BANDS (to Infinity), CAVE_PASSES, loot and ore formulas, ghost bands, habitat geography: all rows and formulas already.
  • Docs own intent, tables own truth. floors.md is the catalog; this page is the inputs checklist.

What does not scale - updated

  • Terrain identity is still branch code. undergroundTerrain hard-codes depths 1/2/3/4; lava is a constant + block in loadCaveTile; chest sources branch on depth 3. Address: the FLOOR_PROFILES row - one row per depth owning terrain mode, biome label, chest source, lava/street-mirror/landing flags - a behavior-preserving refactor that turns every later depth shift into a row edit.
  • Three looks, eight floors. The biome ternary still caps at cave/deep_stone/underdark; floors 4+ render plain. Address: profile.biome + the target labels (goblin city, haunted city, dragon).
  • Floor-locked features are scattered. Seals are table-driven now, but pressure plates, spring caves, the street mirror, splashes and the infernal gate each keep a depth literal in their own module. Address: fold into the profile row (each module reads WorldGen.floorProfile(depth)).
  • Authored areas are singletons. One geometry (spring) at one depth; the goblin city needs a generator, not a flag. Address: the goblin-city feature itself.
  • Elevator stops are finite - and that is by design (a physical lift with parts lanes); the array is already data. Extending it is a row plus a parts lane, nothing structural.

Verdict

Adding depth is cheap; adding floor types now has a named path. The gates-and-seals work proved the pattern: one owning table, three modules reading it, regression tests pinning it. The FLOOR_PROFILES row is the same move applied to terrain and floor flags - it converts the remaining ⚠ rows in section 4 into ✓ rows and makes the floors.md ladder (goblin city, haunted city, dragons) a matter of editing rows rather than forks.
