Dragon Hood  (formerly Mending Lane / TerraCart / Pocket Acres; the neighbourhood is still Mending Lane; storage keys stay `terracart.*`)

A gps driven farming rpg. Inspiration from Pokemon Go and Core keeper/Stardew valley

This is the design spec: what the game does and why. It is NOT the authority on
numbers or invariants — the code is, and CLAUDE.md's QC rules are. Where this
file and either of them disagree, this file is the stale one: fix it.
Numbers, weights, prices, per-tier tables and item lists live in code; each
section points at its owning file and symbol, in backticks.

Core game loop.
1. Player should be rewarded for exploring a large area and finding objects which often correspond to real world map POIs.
2. Economic progression via farming, trading, hunting, exploring.
3. Safety - only encourage the player to go places that are actually safe to walk (avoid the need to walk on roads, water, private property)

Game objects:

# cells
The game is made of a grid of cells generated from vector tile data.
 - Some cells are tillable to replace with tilled soil.
 - grassland biome cells till in half the time.
 - roads should never have any interactables on them directly (only walkable
   paths, or sidewalk beside road). The spawn gate that enforces this is in
   CLAUDE.md ("Generation, saves and tiles").

# viewport
 - the player sees a small square viewport centred on them (`util.js`
   `VIEW_CELLS`). Interaction REACH is a separate, smaller radius — see
   MOVEMENT & REACH.
 - only update or consider timers of cells near the player. Offscreen cells
   keep the timestamp of their last event (a crop keeps its planted time) and
   catch up when seen.

# interactables
Each cell may contain one interactable object (excluding fauna). ie, rocks, trees, crops.
 - timed actions use the WORK WHEEL; a matching tool relic speeds them. Trees
   and ore rocks also have a required tool tier (see MOVEMENT & REACH).
 - state changes should have some visual or text indication.
 - other than house icons and fauna, interactables should fit in a single cell
   (it should be clear which cell they are "in").

# fauna
Entities that can typically move around over cells, like pets, enemies, wild animals
 - crows move in pauses and short straight glides. Ravens (a coin thief) and
   gulls (a food thief) are the crow's body in other colours; each steals once
   per bird per UTC day, then is sated and flies off.
 - SPEED CEILING, shiny speed and the hunted crow's retreat hop: CLAUDE.md
   ("Generation, saves and tiles"; `creature_ai.js` `WILD_SPEED_CEILING_MPS`,
   `CROW_DEPART_HOP`).
 - no fauna may move onto a building footing, or a Major or Medium road; Small
   roads and paths are crossable. None is SEATED on any road: every spawner
   goes through `WorldGen.isSpawnCell`.
 - animals simulate only inside a SIM BUBBLE around the player's feet
   (`creature_ai.js` `CREATURE_SIM_CELLS`), deliberately larger than the
   viewport so a creature is already moving before it comes into view.
   Measured from the player, never the camera anchor — a peek drag wakes
   nothing.
 - CROP RAIDERS: the deer and the crow (`SpriteLayout.CREATURE_BEHAVIOUR`
   `raidsCrops`) notice crops they may eat and go for them. Neither touches a
   potato (`Crops.raiderEats`). The deer bolts from a nearby player, so a
   field is safe while you stand in it; dogs hunt deer, cats hunt crows,
   scarecrows turn both. Deer raid among the houses; crows keep the open
   grass and groves.
 - the slime is a pest that drifts toward the player and leeches energy when
   adjacent; it can be tamed only with a sapphire (the one secret, hinted only
   in the Book's closing riddle).
 - DERELICT LAIRS (`src/lairs.js`, in both modes): an unclaimed structure may
   hold a garrison. What it holds and how many come only from the building
   itself — its tier and a world-fixed strength drawn from its own identity —
   never from Home, the save or tile-load order, so two players at the same
   ruin meet the same monsters. A wrecked house is squatted by slimes; a fort
   or castle is held by goblins.
   Whether a ruin is held at all is drawn from the same stream, so an empty
   ruin stays empty; a tile-wide cap keeps a dense city from being one
   garrison per house (`Lairs.OCCUPANCY`, `Lairs.tileThin`). Easy mode wakes
   only the first few guards of any garrison, the same ones hard meets first
   (`difficulty.js` `lairGuardMax`).
   A garrison HOLDS its seat (so the ruin reads as held from across the
   street), HUNTS when the player comes near, and RETURNS when the player
   escapes, without biting on the way home. Both rings are measured from the
   ruin, so the garrison moves as one and the chase is bounded
   (`Lairs.guardState`). Garrisons are WOKEN around the player, not spawned at
   tile build (`Lairs.stepResidency`), and are generated, never stored: only
   killed guards reach the save. Authored groups are rows of `Lairs.GROUPS`
   (CLAUDE.md "Shared design rules"); the full rationale is the header of
   `src/lairs.js`.
 - THE PEST PUMP (hard mode, `difficulty.js` `cropPests`): a planted crop with
   no wild deer near dispatches one just off-screen at most once an hour of
   wall time (`creature_ai.js` `PEST_DISPATCH_MS`, kept in the save as
   `pestDispatchAt`, so a reload does not restart it), so farming on hard is
   not an unattended income. Near Home, where only one wild deer is admitted
   (`EnemySpawns.refreshHomeFauna`), the dispatched deer takes that slot. On easy only the deer and crows a tile spawns raid.
 - wild deer and crow are GAME; equipped-weapon taps start a hunt using
   the BUG NET's timed wheel, while fed animals can be caught (see CREATURES).
 - feed an animal its favourite food (`items.js` `ANIMAL_FOOD`) before
   catching it. Only catching completes the pet bond.
 - pets keep individual state while carried, following or staying Home.
 - pets recover energy gradually; favourite food fills their energy.

# Terrain and landcover
Source landcover and landuse polygons rasterize to terrain cells according to rules

## Residential
 - Small roads surrounded by housing.
 - Avoid interactables or fauna on private property: yards and lots with no
   public frontage are refused by the spawn gate (CLAUDE.md, BEHIND_HOUSE /
   PRIVATE).

================================================================================
ONBOARDING (the first session)
================================================================================
 - OPENING: two story slides (trailer → wrecked neighbourhood), then the safety
   splash + "Go to my location". On a brand-new save the first GPS fix triggers
   a reload to anchor the projection, so nothing shown before it survives.
 - THE ONE NEIGHBOUR: on a new save the safe area's WARDEN (`npc.js`
   `STORY_ROLES`, seated by `Starter.placeSafeAreaWarden`) is the only person
   on screen. The family's plea is what the warden SAYS ON A TAP — never
   splashed over the map. Everyone else comes back as memories are recovered:
   the story neighbours at their role's memory threshold, ordinary residents
   beside a restored house (or just outside Home's ring until one is theirs),
   off screen, never watched
   appearing (`NPC.arrivals`, `NPC.tickArrivals`). Nexus keepers never left.
   CLAUDE.md ("Dialogs, feedback and teaching") owns the rule.
 - HOW-TO-PLAY CARD: shown once over the LIVE map on first arrival (after that
   reload), covering movement, reach, the farming loop, energy and home-only
   selling. Reopenable from ☰ "How to play".
 - OBJECTIVE CHIP: a HUD chip naming the ONE next thing to do (`quests.js`
   `STARTER_CHAIN` — open a crate → till → plant → restore a wreck → harvest
   → sell at Home). Steps auto-advance on gameplay events fired from the sites
   that perform them (`scene.questEvent`) and each pays a small reward. The
   chip retires when the ladder finishes or is dismissed (☰ "Show objectives"
   brings it back). Hard mode has no ladder (`difficulty.js`).
   The starter ladder is INDEPENDENT of castle quests and citadel garrisons.
   It never opens a castle.
 - STARTER COMPASS: a light-green edge arrow (the tutorial's colour) toward
   the ACTIVE step's own space (`app.js` `_starterGuidanceGoal`): the nearest
   crate, the plot, the soil, a wreck, the crop, Home. When a step needs
   something the bag lacks (a seed, the restore price) it points back at the
   crates. The arrow must never lead away from what the chip asks for.
 - THE STARTER TRAIL: four supply crates are laid along the walk from spawn to
   the relic chest, packed into its near part (`starter.js` `TRAIL_SPAN`) so
   all four are met early. Each stop is in view from the one before. A road
   near the anchor takes the trail onto its kerb (the chip says "along the
   road"); otherwise it follows a walkable route that bends round water and
   buildings. Contents run in the LADDER's order of need, nearest first —
   seeds before the stone and wood a rebuild needs. Crates seat only on legal
   ground, never the road band or the trailer's moat.
 - THE RELIC CHEST: one real treasure chest a screen from spawn, on a cell the
   anchor can walk to, holding ONE random wooden relic — the only thing in the
   opening that hands out a TOOL. Derived from the frozen spawn anchor, so a
   rebuild reproduces it; never a downgrade (`rarity.js`
   `reconcileRelicOffer`).
 - NO PESTS AT HOME UNTIL THE FIRST HARVEST (easy mode, `difficulty.js`
   `pestAmnesty`): until `save.hasHarvested`, no pest is seated near the
   starting anchor (`PEST_FREE_CELLS`). The opening is the stretch with no
   weapon and an empty bag; a pest on the tutorial plot interrupts the ladder
   rather than teaching a fight. A spawn rule, not a cull, baked at tile build.

================================================================================
CORE
================================================================================
 - fullscreen mobile web first (desktop too), a phone-shaped column centred in
   the browser.
 - vector map tiles from OpenFreeMap (OpenMapTiles schema, no API key),
   decoded by hand (`mvt.js`) and cached in IndexedDB; fetched through
   `fetchTileResponse` (CLAUDE.md).
 - Phaser 3 renders the world to a canvas. The view centres on a CAMERA ANCHOR
   — the player plus any peek drag (see INTERACTION MODEL).
 - graceful network loss: a 'reconnecting' banner; cached tiles still serve,
   and a service worker (`sw.js`) precaches the app shell.
 - EXTERNAL MAP DATA IS CACHED AGGRESSIVELY AND NEVER EXPIRES. A cached tile
   is served forever; age only decides whether to ALSO refresh in the
   background, and a failed refresh leaves the cached copy as it was. Only the
   menu's "Reset this game" clears the tile stores.
 - a tile whose build FAILS is not remembered as failed: the half-built entry
   is dropped so a later pass retries.
 - a tile already on screen is never taken away to be rebuilt: the replacement
   is built alongside and swapped in only when ready; a failed rebuild leaves
   the original. Nothing reverts to grass mid-play.
 - in-memory tiles are LRU-capped; an evicted tile rebuilds from cached bytes,
   and per-tile player state lives in `save.*` so the rebuild reconstructs the
   same view.
 - all progress lives in one `save` object persisted to localStorage
   (`save.js`). Tiles cache separately in IndexedDB.

================================================================================
MOVEMENT & REACH
================================================================================
 - GPS drives the player on mobile; WASD / arrows on desktop. The first key
   press latches a manual override for the session.
 - ONE movement model at every depth: a GPS fix (plus any stick offset) sets a
   TARGET and the body walks toward it — underground mining through whatever
   blocks it.
 - a GPS crosshair (`gpsGhost`) marks your REAL position whenever the body has
   left it, so you can see where you are and walk back.
 - the MOVEMENT STICK is always on screen and needs no relic (see STICK
   WALKING under GEAR).
 - device compass drives a facing arrow; with no compass it falls back to the
   last move direction.
 - REACH: you can only interact with cells within your reach radius, measured
   from the player's FEET, never the camera anchor. The lit area, the cell-tap
   gate and every object/creature gate read the same test (`coords.js`
   `reachRadiusM`, `cellInReach`), so "if it's lit, you can tap it". THE LIGHT
   IS THE AFFORDANCE: no reach outline.
 - LIGHTING is ONE pass (`src/lighting.js`; sources are rows of
   `Lighting.KINDS`). CLAUDE.md ("Lighting and streets") owns the rules;
   `tools/layer_audit.js` pins the layer order.
 - REACH SIZE (`coords.js` `reachCells`): grows with the wizard's Inner Light
   rungs (`save.reachUpgrades`), shrinks with underground depth. Being tired does NOT
   shrink reach — the interaction reach display flushes red as energy runs low — but at 0
   energy there is no reach at all. A Potion of Reach opens the whole view
   for its duration.
 - WORK WHEEL: every timed action (chop / mine / fish / pick a worked crop /
   catch or hunt an animal) shows a progress wheel at the spot. The matching
   tool's TIER sets the duration on ONE geometric ladder shared by every tool
   (`items.js` `TOOL_DURATION_MS`, `toolDurationMs`); bare hands are a slower
   floor below the first rung, so work is always possible. The ladder is
   geometric so every upgrade reads as an upgrade.
   TOOL GATES: trees and ore rocks have a required tool tier (tree size and
   species, ore tier). Exactly one tier short offers a slow, costly grind;
   further short is refused; an under-tooled target draws faded
   (`interactables.js` `runInteractable`, `isToolGated`, `SLOW_GRIND_MS`).
   Fishing is the exception to the ladder (see FISHING). Tapping anywhere
   cancels a wheel and refunds any pre-charged energy; the launching tap
   cannot cancel it.
 - COMBAT shows no wheel — the target wears a HEALTH BAR. Weapon damage rates
   derive from the same work ladder (see COMBAT).

================================================================================
ENERGY & FOOD
================================================================================
 - energy is the one pool (`save.energy`, written only by `Energy.set`) spent
   on hard actions and shown as ⚡cur/max with a traffic-light colour.
 - job costs (`items.js` `ENERGY_COST`, and each interactable's `energy`): the
   tool jobs cost less with a better matching tool; picking wildplants is
   free.
 - ARMOUR does NOT raise max energy — it SOAKS blows (`Combat.mitigate`,
   `Combat.playerDamage`): each worn piece adds its tier to a pool spent
   against each hit with diminishing returns, and a hit always lands at least
   a little. Routing and ordering rules are CLAUDE.md's ("Combat, energy and
   Home").
 - max energy rises two ways (`Energy.maxEnergy`): a FIRST-TASTE bonus for
   every distinct edible ever eaten (`save.eaten`) and the wizard's VIGOUR
   rungs (`save.vigourUpgrades`).
 - EAT: a persistent Eat button eats the selected food (`FOOD_ENERGY`). Pets
   are never edible.
 - special foods have side effects (a rainberry waters nearby crops, a pairy
   points at the nearest undiscovered chest, coffee hurries the stick); their
   timers are rows of `Buffs.KINDS` (CLAUDE.md status row rule).
 - REST: energy regenerates in a rest context and pauses while you are
   WORKING (`energy.js`; `app.js` owns the per-frame accrual):
     * HOME — Home's ring (`HOME_R`) rests you fastest. THE ONLY BUILDING THAT
       RESTS YOU. Home is a campfire you own: the same ring lights, rests and
       wards (CLAUDE.md).
     * CAMPFIRE — a lit fire's ring (`FIRE_REST_R`) rests slowly, anywhere.
     * OFFLINE — the bar refills pro rata while the tab is closed
       (`Energy.OFFLINE_FULL_REST_MS`).
 - EXHAUSTION: hitting 0 energy UNDERGROUND blacks the player out and wakes
   them on the surface, costing half their coins (never negative); they wake
   drained and cannot go back down until they rest (`app.js`
   `_passOutToSurface`). On the surface 0 energy only blocks energy-costing
   actions — except on hard, where it costs the same half purse.

================================================================================
WORLD GENERATION
================================================================================
 - each MVT tile is rasterized ONCE into a grid of cells (`WorldGen.CELL_M`,
   one terrain class per cell), then decorated with objects, debris,
   creatures and treasure. Deterministic per tile, so the same place always
   generates the same world (identity rules: CLAUDE.md).
 - terrain cell classes group into three base biomes; each subtype gets its own
   colour so the world reads varied but cohesive:
     ROCKY/URBAN: residential, commercial (retail/hospital), industrial, road,
                  path, building tiers, pier
     FOREST:      forest, orchard, wetland
     GRASSLAND:   grass, park, school grounds, sports pitch, playground, golf,
                  farmland (allotments lumped in)
   plus water and sand. Road terrain splits into Small / Medium / Major and Path; buildings
   into house / fort / castle.
 - building tier comes from footprint area and height; each building places
   one house/tower sprite anchored on its footprint.
 - BUILDING FOOTPRINTS NEVER OVERLAP. Each polygon is assigned an exclusive
   set of cells before anything is painted, decided by how much of each cell
   each polygon covers, never by paint order — so a building clipped by a tile
   seam is shaped the same in both tiles. A shed smaller than a cell still
   gets one.
 - every tile enforces a minimum building mix so trade and landmarks always
   exist (every tile with a few buildings has at least one of each tier).
 - flat colour is drawn first, then tileset terrain with auto-tiled edges.
 - building polygons → house/tower sprite; forest → trees; orchard → fruit
   trees; useful POIs → chests; fountains → wells; plus rocks, debris,
   treasure and creatures. A satextract sidecar (`data/`) adds extra real POIs
   (trees, fountains, street furniture).
 - NO SCATTER OBJECT SITS ON (OR BESIDE) A BUILDING: worldgen keeps a one-cell
   moat around every building cell. The Home trailer is synthetic, so it gets
   the same moat at runtime (`app.js` `clearHomeTrailerOverlap`); real
   buildings and ground flora are spared.
 - spawn gating, precedence and influence zones: CLAUDE.md ("Generation, saves
   and tiles", "Spawn precedence").

================================================================================
INTERACTION MODEL
================================================================================
 - EVERYTHING is tap-driven. Walking never changes a cell. A tap does EXACTLY
   ONE thing: handlers are tried in priority order (`interact.js`
   `TAP_HANDLERS`) and the first that matches consumes the tap.
 - A TAP IS A POINTER THAT DIDN'T TRAVEL. Dragging PEEKS: the camera slides off
   the player a little and springs back on release. A pointer that moved taps
   NOTHING — sliding the map off a tree must not chop it. The peek is a camera
   offset only: reach, tap gates, fog and tile loading still measure from the
   player.
 - TAP TARGETS ARE CELL-BOUNDED: everything except fauna is hit by the one
   cell it stands in, however tall its art (CLAUDE.md "Coordinates, rendering
   and performance"). Creatures are tapped by their drawn sprite. A tap on any
   building cell routes to that building.
 - the auto-till fallthrough never tills a cell occupied by something, so a
   near-miss never silently tills under it.
 - routine actions flash briefly; bigger loot pops are rarity-coloured. Map
   copy and toast tiers: CLAUDE.md ("Dialogs, feedback and teaching").

================================================================================
FARMING LOOP
================================================================================
 - all farming is tap-driven; tools are implicit (a Hoe only helps).
     1) tap untilled tillable cell → TILL.
     2) tap tilled empty cell with a seed selected → PLANT. With no seed
        selected the tap only says so — soil is NEVER un-tilled by tapping
        it. (That toggle existed until Aug 2026 and fired on exactly the tap a
        beginner makes, handing back the plot they had just paid for.)
     3) tap planted dry cell → WATER.
     4) a watered stage advances after a hold, even while away
        (`Crops.STAGE_HOLD_MS`).
     5) tap mature crop → HARVEST: produce, the plant is gone, a chance of a
        bonus seed.
 - any tillable cell (non-water, non-building, unoccupied) can be tilled.
 - PRODUCE QUALITY IS THE BED'S, AND THE HOE SETS IT. Tilling banks the hoe's
   tier on the cell; planting spends it onto the crop, and quality raises the
   bonus-seed chance and the yield at harvest (`Crops.bedQuality`). A tame
   butterfly pollinating a crop gives it some quality.
 - WATERING CAN: HOW SOON, not what — a better can has a better chance of
   springing the crop a whole stage on watering (`Crops.waterJumpChance`).
 - per-cell state lives in `save.tilled`, `save.tilledQuality`, `save.planted`.

================================================================================
FORAGING / WILD PICKUPS
================================================================================
 - DEBRIS: residential / park / forest / grassland / beach polygons get a
   stable seeded density of one fitting wild crop (mushrooms in yards, shrubs
   in parks and forest, shells on beaches, ferns on grassland).
 - tap a wild plant within reach to pick it. Some need WORK: rubble (stone)
   via the Pick, shrubs (woody — they yield wood) via the Axe.
 - SURPRISE TREASURE: some picks have a small chance of a bonus drop.
 - NEST BUSHES: see EGG HATCHING.
 - picked state persists in `save.picked` (stable per-cell id).

================================================================================
MINING
================================================================================
 - all mining is via MINERAL ROCKS — clusters on quarry / industrial /
   residential / scree polygons. A plain rock is open to bare hands and pays
   stone with a chance of more; an ORE rock pays coal and its tier's bar, the
   higher ones a gem, and needs a pick near its tier (see the tool gate in
   MOVEMENT & REACH; drops: `interactables.js` `mineralrock`).
 - density by zone: residential sparse and low-tier; industrial dense mid-tier;
   rock landcover dense, reaching the top tiers but weighted low.
 - broken rocks persist (`save.brokenRocks`).
 - rubble can be PLACED as a stone "fence" on an empty cell and picked back
   up.

================================================================================
CHOPPING
================================================================================
 - TREES chop via the Axe work wheel and drop wood. Chopped state persists
   (`save.chopped`); stumps don't block tilling.
 - ACORNS: a fell may leave an acorn, a sapling that plants a new timber tree,
   so a wood you clear is a wood you can replant. The chance is the AXE's only
   reward besides speed and cheaper swings (`items.js` `acornDropChance`).
   Planted, it grows a choppable `tree` (not a fruit tree) on the clock
   (`util.js` `treeGrowthStage`) — one function, so the drawn size, the axe
   gate and the wood paid all climb together.
 - TREE SIZE & SPECIES (`util.js`): a tree's drawn size, its wood yield and
   the axe tier it needs all derive from the tree's own fields (no extra save
   state), so the visual and gameplay sizes never diverge
   (`treeAxeReqTier`). Softwood fells a tier easier, hardwood a tier harder;
   wood tracks size. Shiny trees need a strong axe and pay the shiny bonus.
 - HOME GROVE: trees near spawn are forced to softwood
   (`HomeArea.softwoodSpeciesNear`) so the opening wood economy is reliably
   harvestable bare-handed.

================================================================================
ORCHARD FRUIT TREES
================================================================================
 - orchard landcover spawns fruit trees that bear on a timer; tapping a ripe
   tree picks the fruit and resets it. Never chopped. Fruit type is seeded
   from the cell.
 - a BEARING tree wears its fruit's own icon on the canopy as a separate
   sprite; the tree's art never changes, so a worked orchard still reads as an
   orchard and what's ripe reads at a glance.

================================================================================
EGG HATCHING
================================================================================
 - EGGS incubate with real GPS walking while the game is open
   (`items.js` `EGG_HATCH_METERS`); stick or unreliable travel does not count.
   Selecting an egg shows Eat and a Hatch action with the distance left. A
   hatch puts a random BABY PET in the bag (`BABY_KINDS`).
 - NEST BUSHES: a few shrubs (`SHINY_RATE.nest`, by the bush's id — the same
   bushes for everyone) wiggle now and then and, chopped, hand over a baby.
 - Released, a baby is a tame pet that grows up after a while
   (`SpriteLayout.isBabyPet`). A pet raised from a baby is ALWAYS SHINY and,
   grown, stronger than its kind (`raisedMul`); shiny and adult bonuses do not
   stack (CLAUDE.md).

================================================================================
FISHING
================================================================================
 - THE STARTER POND: no spawn promises water, so every save gets a small pond
   carved a walk from Home (`app.js` `_carveStarterPond`), beside a POI chest
   when one is in the band, only where the anchor can walk to, never on a road
   or a plaza. Frozen in `save.starterPondAt` and repainted on every rebuild;
   its cells are always stocked. Pinned by `starter_pond.test.js`.
 - FISH LIVE IN SECRET SPOTS (Sep 2026): a water cell holds one fish or none,
   hashed off the cell (`items.js` `fishSpotStocked`), so every player has the
   same spots and nothing on screen marks them. Landing a fish empties the
   spot forever (`save.fishedSpots`). An empty cast may still turn up junk, a
   slime or treasure (`rollEmptyCast`). A stocked spot always bites: the
   scarcity is the spots, not the strike.
 - Cast TIME is locked — bare hands slow, any rod fast, regardless of tier (an
   exception to the WORK WHEEL ladder: tier-scaled casts made high-tier fishing
   an unbounded money faucet).
 - WHICH FISH is fixed per spot (`spotFish`, weighted by `FISH_SPECIES`). A
   rod at or above the fish's tier always lands it; below, it may get away and
   stays for the next cast (`fishCatchChance`). Rod tier also cheapens the
   cast; fishing costs more energy than other tool jobs (`FISH_COST_MULT`).
   Pinned by `test/node/fishing.test.js`.

================================================================================
CREATURES
================================================================================
 - spawned per tile from winning landcover/Nexus/Road Variant profiles
   (`HabitatSpawns`); ordinary roads and paths add no population. Safety
   excludes seats; creatures use other legal ground in the same habitat.
   Evening busy-road suppression hides ambient inhabitants without replacement.
   Only simulated near the player. Domestic, wild and pest kinds; behaviour is a
   row of `SpriteLayout.CREATURE_BEHAVIOUR`.
 - Inside the surface Home circle (250 m from the starter anchor), wild
   animals are limited to chickens and at most one living deer. Owned pets
   are exempt. This is a per-save presence filter, including animals that
   wander in; generated identities and positions are preserved.
 - FEED THEN CATCH: favourite food makes that individual catchable, without
   bonding it. A catch wheel finishes the bond; a net speeds the wheel. Fed
   slimes are caught by tapping. Unfed animals cannot be caught.
 - One owned animal per species, including carried and deployed pets. Release
   the current individual before catching another. Variants share species.
 - Plant produce fed to a producer gives its species yield on a per-animal
   cooldown. Other food on a wild animal is refused.
 - Unfed GAME (crow / deer) can still be deliberately hunted with an equipped
   weapon and empty hand. The hunt uses the Bug Net's timed work wheel. Fed
   game animals follow the ordinary catching rules.
 - Owned pets open their individual menu on tap. Carrying and setting down
   preserve identity, colour, growth, energy, bonuses and accessories. Release
   relinquishes ownership and returns accessories.
 - Favourite food restores full energy. A pet at zero energy rests upside
   down for one minute, then returns with one energy; it regenerates slowly.
 - Eggs and nest discoveries reveal wild babies. They still need favourite
   food and catching before becoming pets.
 - SCARECROW: a placeable item that wild crows and deer steer around.
 - SLIMES are hostile when wild. Sapphire prepares a slime for catching;
   feeding it alone does not make it a pet.

================================================================================
COMBAT
================================================================================
 - ENEMIES are the things that attack YOU (`Combat.isEnemy`): the surface and
   cave roster (`enemy_roster.js` `EnemyRoster.ROWS`, built into
   `Combat.MONSTERS`; previewed by `tools/monster-roster.html`). Crow and deer
   are game; a tamed slime is a pet and never a target.
 - HIT POINTS: every enemy has HP; damage from any source drains the one pool,
   and a foe left alone heals back after a while. HP is in-memory.
 - ONLY ONE WEAPON FIGHTS AT A TIME (`save.activeWeapon`, `Gear.activeWeapon`).
   MELEE IS THE DEFAULT and needs no weapon: with no ranged weapon in hand,
   the hands (or the sword) auto-engage the nearest enemy in reach
   (`Gear.meleeActive`). A newly obtained or upgraded weapon becomes active;
   switching is an explicit Equip / Unequip, and the active weapon wears an
   "E" badge. Melee always follows the closest eligible foe; an auto-engaged
   fight never swallows taps, roots the character or blocks the walk home.
 - STATUS EFFECTS are rows of `Conditions.DEFINITIONS` (poison, burning, …):
   player, foe, HUD chip and tint all derive from the row (CLAUDE.md).
 - WEAPONS DIFFER IN KIND — reach, cadence, aim and ammunition (`RELIC_DEFS`,
   `Combat.SHOT`):
     sword   — melee; drains HP on the combat wheel.
     dagger / lance — alternate melee slots, chest finds only: the dagger
               shorter, the lance longer and slower on foot.
     bow     — an arrow along the COMPASS heading: aiming is turning. It
               stops in the first foe and at anything solid. Spends wood.
     staff   — a magic bolt that SEEKS the nearest foe in range, pierces every
               foe on its line and ignores the world. Spends energy; its range
               is the player's own reach.
     musket  — a ball along the compass; each shot spends a coin.
   Ranged weapons wake only for a foe close to the player's reach (owner, Sep
   2026: a seeking weapon firing at anything on screen cleared the street at
   no risk). A slot's cadence is pacing, not a nerf: each shot is priced at
   its own interval, so the delivered rate holds (`Combat.SHOT`
   `fireIntervalMs`).
 - HEALTH IS A BAR, NOT A RING: the engaged foe wears a health bar seated on
   its crown, recently hurt foes a dimmer one, and damage pops over the foe
   (CLAUDE.md). The work wheel's ring is for jobs only.
 - A CLAIMED CASTLE'S TURRETS FIGHT FOR YOU (`Combat.TURRET`, `app.js`
   `_turretFire`), firing the player's own Wood-tier arrow at a slower rate.
   The claim gate is the same `isClaimedKey` test that decides the tower's
   unclaimed palette and its light, so a castle that reads dead never shoots.
 - DEFEATING AN ENEMY PAYS COINS — every enemy. The bounty is derived from the
   kind's HP and depth (`Combat.enemyBounty`), so a tougher foe never pays
   less. Some cave kills also roll treasure. Game pays in feathers and meat.
 - EVERY route to a dead creature pays the same — the hunt wheel, the combat
   wheel, a shot, a pet's kill — because they all funnel through
   `resolveDefeat`.

================================================================================
CHESTS (POI-driven)
================================================================================
 - useful real-world POIs (shops, food, civic, healthcare, parks, sports,
   culture, street furniture, …) each spawn a chest at the POI, snapped to the
   grid and deduplicated by name.
 - tap a chest within reach to open it. Its TIER is its tile's quota seat
   (`WorldGen.seedChestTiers`; the deeper caps are `loot.js`
   `chestTierMaxFor`) — the full rule is CLAUDE.md ("Generation, saves and
   tiles"). What it holds is the POI's chest theme (`chest_themes.js`
   `ChestThemes`, keyed by POI category) rolled through `rarity.js`
   `pickReward`. Some opens hand out gear instead (see GEAR).
 - Chests give ONCE (`save.opened`; CLAUDE.md). An opened chest of any tier
   VANISHES from the map; its pad and label persist.
 - chest TIER is shown by recolouring the approved chest sprite in the rarity
   badge palette; higher tiers glow in their rarity colour while unopened. No
   floating tier gem.
 - BAG-FULL: "Take what fits" (chest emptied) or "Leave for later" (the exact
   roll is remembered in `save.chestHold`, so there is no re-rolling for
   something better).
 - every UNOPENED POI BREATHES LIGHT (a `Lighting.KINDS` row), and that light
   IS the marker: never draw a ring back.
 - every POI's concrete pad is tinted an electric light blue — the one surface
   that carries "a place lives here" on its own. Minor POIs get a smaller,
   dimmer pad; loose supply crates get none.
 - POI names are RUSTICIFIED for display (`loot.js` `RUSTIC_WORDS`) and
   tinted the treasure blue; only loose supply crate labels stay plain white.

================================================================================
BREAKABLE POTS AND BARRELS
================================================================================
 - Waste baskets, recycling points and generated breakables choose a stable
   barrel or clay-pot appearance, and that appearance selects their loot
   (`loot.js` `BARREL_ART` / `barrelProfile`); each smash gives at most one
   item and usually nothing. Exclusive finds stay in their own sources.
 - Both give ONCE (`save.opened`) and stay as broken art; they are not on the
   crate restock ledger.

================================================================================
COIN-BURST POIs (ATM / bicycle parking)
================================================================================
 - An ATM is a POT OF GOLD: once a UTC day a tap scatters coins on walkable
   cells nearby, fewer where ATMs are common (`loot.js`
   `potCoinsFor`). Coins never land on a road or in a yard and stay on the
   player's side of a Major or Medium road (CLAUDE.md).
 - A bike rack (a "Courier's Post" in the world) is not a chest: once a UTC day
   it lends the movement stick extra pace for a while (`loot.js` `isBikeRack`,
   `items.js` `BIKE_RACK_SPEED_MUL`).
 - Both are recurring sites on the one day ledger (`Macros.markToday`) and
   glow while available (CLAUDE.md).

================================================================================
TREASURE MARKS (X on the ground)
================================================================================
 - claim by tapping the cell the X is drawn on (within reach). Reward via the
   unified rarity picker — mostly small, sometimes better, rarely rare. Found
   state persists in `save.foundTreasures`.
 - sources: the starter trail (see ONBOARDING; fixed contents, no rarity
   roll); a guaranteed X at every parking lot; a few random marks per tile;
   extra marks beside FOOTPATHS and ON SAND. Residential X's only spawn near a
   road.
 - THE BEACH STREAM: sand gets its own stream seated on the sand (a beach is
   dug), capped per area of sand (`BEACH_X_PER_CELLS`) so a sandpit gets a
   mark while a shoreline gets the roll.
 - both bonus streams share ONE walk of the grid, packing cells by grid index.
   Pinned by `test/node/beach_treasure.test.js`.

================================================================================
STREET RESTORATION (the road band itself)
================================================================================
 - THE ROAD BAND IS DILAPIDATED BY DEFAULT and the player rebuilds it. Wherever
   a stretch has sat inside the player's lit reach CONTINUOUSLY for a short
   dwell (`app.js` `PATH_STONE_DWELL_MS`) it turns to clean cobble, forever,
   saved. Footpaths restore too. Rail and parking aisles never
   (`WorldGen.isParkingAisle` — an aisle is not a street).
 - A STRETCH IS METRES, NOT CELLS: an interval of arclength along ONE LINE of
   one way, keyed by `Streets.lineKey` (never the feature alone — merged
   multi-lines would weld unrelated streets) and clipped to the tile square
   (`Streets.tileSpans`) so a way crossing a seam is never paid twice. The
   terrain grid under-reports roads, so a per-cell rule could never match what
   the player sees. CLAUDE.md ("Lighting and streets") owns the invariants.
 - THE SWEEP (`app.js` `_sweepStreets`) scans what is in reach from the
   player's reach cell (never the camera anchor) and ripens what has been in
   sight for the whole dwell (`Streets.createSight`). Sight is CONTINUOUS:
   clipping the bubble in passing restarts the clock. The auto-walk home, a
   cave and a flat battery bank nothing — that is the game moving the body.
 - THE SAVE: `save.streets` (restored intervals per tile and line),
   `save.streetsEpoch` (bumped on any restore; folded into the redraw key so
   only a restore repaints) and `save.trail` (the prize ladder).
 - DRAWING: the dilapidated network and the restored stretches are baked
   canvases; a live layer carries the DWELL PREVIEW (a pale light arriving,
   not a stain creeping in) and the SHINE down a stretch the moment it comes
   back (`road_overlay.js`). Feather only the restored band's edge; never blur
   the drawn layer.
 - THE LAMPS: A RESTORED STREET LIGHTS ITS OWN WAY — a street lamp every so
   many metres of rebuilt carriageway (`Streets.lampSpacingM`; independent of
   the prize ladder, so an ordinary short block still earns one). It is the
   reason to rebuild a street after dark, and the string of lamps behind you
   is the map of everything you have restored.
     * GENERATED, NEVER STORED: placed from the line's own geometry
       (`Streets.lampsAlong`), lit when covered by restored intervals
       (`Streets.covers`); nothing new reaches the save.
     * SPREAD, NOT STAMPED FROM ZERO: evenly across each line with half an
       interval at each end, so short ways, OSM splits and junctions don't
       bunch lamps; lamps outside the tile square are dropped.
     * ART AND LIGHT ON ONE POINT: the lamp is baked art (it must read at
       noon) and a steady `Lighting.KINDS` source, seated on the verge
       (`Streets.lampOffsetM`).
     * AN UNLIT LAMP draws as the old grey pebble cluster, so the lamps to
       light are visible before they light. Dark lamps throw no light.
     * ONE LIST, ONE FLAG feeds both art and light, so they can never
       disagree.
 - THE BLAST (restoration fanfare) is ONE thing at any size (`app.js`
   `_blastAt`): a transient light, debris in the thing's material, sparks,
   and for a street a GATHER of setts pulled back toward the stretch so the
   moment reads as a REPAIR. One per sweep, at the longest restored piece;
   also fired when a wreck is restored. Add a call site, never a light row.
 - ONE LADDER FOR THE WHOLE WORLD: every restored metre anywhere adds to one
   running total, and prizes land on a rising ladder of goals (`trail.js`;
   interval algebra in `streets.js`). A metre is a metre wherever it is
   restored — the old per-path prizes paid the same walk differently depending
   on how OSM split the ways, and none of that survives.
 - each sweep pops a small COUNTER on the stretch just rebuilt (`Trail.label`,
   the one formatter), throttled; the paying sweep reads the goal just
   completed, full, never the remainder against the next rung.
 - each prize IS A CHOICE: two different rolls are offered (`Trail.PRIZE_CHOICES`;
   context `Trail.PRIZE_CONTEXT`) and nothing is granted until one is taken.
   The kept gift opens as its own reward card. Rolls improve gradually as the
   walks get longer (`Trail.rollBonusFor`): the first prize rolls T1, each
   prize won adds `PRIZE_TIER_PER_PRIZE` (0.3) of a tier, so T4 is common by
   prize 10 and sure from prize 11. The first prize's onion-seed card is fixed.
 - A SINGLE EARNED REWARD IS SHOWN AS A CARD (`app.js` `showRewardCard`),
   framed by its giver; finds on the ground stay toasts.
 - WHICH CELLS ARE PATH is geometric: a cell is PATH only where at least a full
   cell width of way runs through it (`worldgen.js` `accumulateLineSpan`,
   summed over every footpath before painting). The diagonal connectivity
   elbow is always painted. A run of too few path cells is not a path and
   dissolves back to its underlying terrain (`WorldGen.MIN_PATH_RUN_CELLS`), unless it
   continues into the neighbour tile.

WELLS / FOUNTAINS
================================================================================
 - OSM fountains become a 'well' object: a water source on dry land with
   flavour text (produce quality moved to the HOE; see FARMING).

================================================================================
ECONOMY (buying & selling)
================================================================================
 - currency = coins (`save.money`); a new save starts with a small purse
   (`STARTING_MONEY`; less on hard). Earn by selling, coin pickups, chests,
   pots of gold, deliveries and enemy bounties.
 - WRECK-HOUSE RESTORATION: every small house starts as a derelict WRECK with
   no shop function (the trailer is exempt — Home works from day one).
   Restoring costs stone, on a ladder that climbs with houses already restored
   (`items.js` `wreckRestoreQty`); other cards are priced by their rank
   (`houses.js` `buildCost`). The dialog's quote is exactly what is charged.
 - WHAT A WRECK BECOMES IS THE PLAYER'S PICK (Oct 2026): cards from ONE table,
   `Houses.BUILD_OPTIONS`, unlocked by restore count (`STORY_RESTORES`): house,
   blacksmith, shop, trader, turret, the pet shop, the book shop, the wizard
   tower and his second tower. Nothing is forced and nothing is read off the OSM address;
   the Blacksmith card is SUGGESTED while the lane has no smithy, since the
   wooden tools come from nowhere else. `Houses.restoreAs` freezes the pick
   and is the ledger's one writer (CLAUDE.md).
   THE OFFER (`Houses.offerCards`): restored + 1 slots — the House always
   (and the wizard's tower while offered), then up to `NEW_SLOTS` (3) cards
   the player has nothing like yet (`Houses.isNewPick`, a NEW pill), then
   buildable duplicates; each pool a window over the catalogue
   (`Houses.buildOptions`) that slides one card along per restore. ANY
   NUMBER of a card may stand. A RANKED card (smithy, shop, trader) is one
   per rank the ladder has unlocked, stamped in `save.shopTiers`. THE LADDER
   CLIMBS BY MEMORIES, never by wrecks (`Shops.tierCap`: T2 at five
   lifetime memories, a rank every `MEMORIES_PER_TIER`, T7 at thirty). A
   RENOVATION PERMIT (`items.js` `renovation_permit`, a T4 supply) held at a
   standing smithy, shop or trader raises it ONE rank (`Houses.renovateTo`
   / `renovate`), only to a rank the ladder has opened and the line still
   allows; a refusal says why on the map.
   A SHOP is a THEMED market, one LINE at one rank, from `Shops.LINE_RULES`:
   seed and supply to T3, the magic shop one per tier to T7, relic to T7;
   there is no ore shop. The PET SHOP and the BOOK SHOP are one-offs
   (`shops.js` `SOLO_LINES`): built once per save, outside that table.
   THE MAGIC HAMMER (`houses.js` `HAMMER_ID`): spent on a restore,
   the building comes up SHINY and sells at a standing discount for good
   (`Houses.priceMul` — the one standing discount a building carries); a
   shiny turret fights harder instead. A plain house takes no hammer
   (`Houses.hammerTakes`).
   Smithies and traders read their RANK off the same stamp (`shops.js`
   `smithTier`, `traderTier`; older houses keep the derivations that raised
   them): a smithy forges near its own rank; a trader's barter leans to
   goods of its rank (`Shops.tierAffinity`). Every
   ranked door wears its rarity badge (`Shops.shopTier`). A TURRET is a lone
   castle tower on a house lot that fights on the castle turret lane. PLAIN
   houses run deliveries. All of these BUY/trade — selling is home-only.
 - FORTS start SEALED; a one-time stack of wood, rising across forts, opens
   one (`houses.js` `FORT_UNLOCK_WOOD_START`). An open fort runs a SLOT
   MACHINE (`presentFortSlots`, odds in `ShopsMath`) that costs what it wins
   on average.
 - CASTLES open individually. On first conversation, each unguarded castle
   receives one permanent quest determined by its variant. Bastions ask for
   three enemies of one type, advancing through enemy types in tier order;
   Archives always ask for one delivery; Weathered Ruins ask for two animals
   of one huntable species,
   cycling through the game-animal list. Assignment order advances each type
   separately. Counters start at first conversation. One delivery or hunt
   advances every matching active quest. Earlier actions never count. Claiming never rerolls
   the job, and there are no shared quest slots or random quest templates.
   The board shows the castle as caption, the quest title in bold, then one
   sentence of flavour (`Quests.flavour`) over the objective and progress.
   Citadel fights expire 10 minutes after activation, including time away.
   On expiry their guards despawn and all battle progress resets for a fresh attempt.
   Citadels offer Fight or Later at their gate. Only choosing Fight activates
   their guards; walking nearby, reading the dialog, or choosing Later leaves
   them dormant. The choice survives reloads; clearing the guards opens them. Other castle
   variants have no guards; house and fort garrisons retain their usual rules.
   Claiming mans the turrets (COMBAT), lights the castle, and opens a castellan
   favour (rest or a coin tax) on the Houses.CASTLE_SERVICE_MS timer.
 - WIZARD TOWER: spends MEMORIES (`save.memories` — see SHINY VARIANTS &
   DISCOVERY) on rungs of power (`src/wizard.js` `Wizard.TRACKS`). Each visit
   offers two tracks, seeded so re-opening shows the same pair:
     * INNER LIGHT — more reach (see MOVEMENT & REACH).
     * FULL MEASURE — QUANTITY luck (`qtyLuck`).
     * KEEN EYE — TIER luck (`upgradeLuck`), the tower's exclusive gift.
     * VIGOUR — the cheap track: more max energy.
   The THIRD purchase is a one-time CALLING (`Wizard.CLASSES`: Hunter,
   Runner, Enforcer, Enchanter). What each rung does lives with the module
   that applies it; printed numbers come from that module, never a copy.
 - OFFER MODAL: item, cost, Buy/Cancel (greyed if unaffordable). Shops may
   offer a paid re-roll whose price climbs (`ShopsMath.themedRerollCost`, `smithyRerollCost`).
 - NO PER-HOUR DEAL CAP (Sep 2026): shops are used continuously. The clock
   eases the re-roll ladder by one rung per hour bucket, never a reset
   (`ShopsMath.easedRerolls`), and the trader alone has a short cooldown after
   a closed barter (`ShopsMath.DEAL_COOLDOWN_MS`) — its goods leave with the
   deal, so an instant reopen converted any stack into any other for free.
   The wait is shown with `shortDuration`, or said with `spokenDuration`.
 - BUY: cash storefronts always price in COINS at a random markup
   (`ShopsMath.buyPrice`, `items.js` `buyMarkupRange`). BARTER is the TRADER's
   job alone. Cash purchases hand over ONE unit; the bundle is barter-only, so
   cash buys can never be resold at a profit. Magical flower seeds are loot
   finds, never on the seed rotation (`BUY_LIST`).
 - SELL: only at HOME (the trailer), at one flat rate (`items.js`
   `trailerSellPrice`; less on hard). NO RELIC BENDS A PRICE (Oct 2026; the
   Sword and Bow used to): standing discounts come only from the hammer or a
   carried guild badge.
 - ROADSIDE STANDS (`loot.js` `produceStandFor`) sell BELOW the listed price —
   a producer selling its own goods — but A STAND MUST NEVER BE AN ARBITRAGE
   PUMP: the stand price stays above the sell rate, capped at par
   (`ShopsMath.standPrice`). The tracking guarantees the invariant, not the
   margin; rounding always favours the stand. `shops_math.test.js` sweeps
   every price.
 - DELIVERIES: a PLAIN house wants a SET of specific produce and pays a premium
   over selling them one by one (`DELIVERY_BONUS_MULT`). A delivered house is
   satisfied for the rest of the UTC day. The wanted tier ramps with your
   lifetime tally (`Delivery.tierCap`): early neighbours ask for potatoes, a
   veteran courier for coconuts. The first houses walk a fixed opening list.
================================================================================
GEAR: RELICS & ARMOR
================================================================================
 - SEVEN MATERIAL TIERS for tiered gear, Wood to Frost (`items.js`
   `MATERIAL_TIERS`); cost and effect climb together. One piece per slot.
 - GEAR REWARDS from any source: an upgrade auto-equips; a non-upgrade is
   cashed out. Always shown in a reward modal.
 - RELIC SLOTS (`items.js` `RELIC_DEFS`):
     pick / axe — speed their work, cheapen it, and meet the tool gates; the
              axe also raises the acorn chance (CHOPPING)
     hoe    — cheaper tilling and the bed's produce QUALITY (FARMING)
     can    — a chance to spring a crop a stage (FARMING)
     rod    — cheaper casts and better landing odds (FISHING)
     bugnet — THE animal tool: speeds the catch and the crow/deer hunt
     bags   — raises the per-item stack cap (`STACK_CAP_BY_TIER`)
     sword / dagger / lance / bow / staff / musket — weapons (COMBAT)
 - RINGS AND AMULETS are untiered UNIQUE RELICS (`items.js` kind
   `unique_relic`, effects in `UNIQUE_JEWELRY`), carried rather than worn in a
   tiered slot. Tier luck is the wizard's Keen Eye, not a ring.
 - ARMOUR SLOTS (helmet / chest / legs / boots, `ARMOR_DEFS`) SOAK blows (see
   ENERGY & FOOD). Boots also blunt traps and set stick-walking pace and cost.
 - STICK WALKING: the movement stick is ALWAYS on screen — no relic and no
   separate mode. It walks the body itself somewhere other than the GPS
   target; the distance is banked as an offset, so each new fix targets GPS +
   offset and ground covered by hand survives it. A GPS jump big enough to be
   a relocation (`app.js` `GPS_SNAP_M`) places the body on the fix outright.
   Underground the body always mines its way over. Walking WITH the GPS is
   free — that's you actually walking. Stick walking costs energy per cell and
   BOOTS set its speed and cost (`items.js` `steerSpeedMul`,
   `steerEnergyCost`); buffs such as Dragon Powder, the Speed potion, coffee
   and a bike rack lend it pace (`Buffs.KINDS`). At 0 energy the stick stops;
   the GPS still moves you.
   Straying is shaped by a ring around your real position: inside it the
   stick is nearly free; outside it you pay full price and the character
   darkens with a pulsing halo. At 0 energy the tint and halo turn red.
   Let the stick sit idle (`WALK_HOME_IDLE_MS`) and the character WALKS
   ITSELF HOME, free, easing in; any push restarts the timer. Surface only,
   never during a work wheel. Past `GPS_SNAP_M` the return is placed, not
   walked: that distance is travel the player never made on foot.
   That delay is a DEBOUNCE, not a pause, and it has been wrong in both
   directions: too long and the character sat frozen after release; too short
   and players nudging themselves along in short pushes were walked BACK
   against them. Playtesting settled past the longest natural gap between
   two deliberate pushes; the ease-in (`WALK_HOME_RAMP_MS`) keeps a generous
   debounce from being a cliff. The lead-line hint (`WALK_HOME_HINT_IDLE_MS`)
   must stay LONGER than the delay — it announces a walk, so it can't
   precede one. `walk_home.test.js` pins both bounds.
 - TIER GATING of rewards: the only gate on rarer gear is each source's own
   loot ceiling (`rarity.js`), so a bus-stop chest can't cough up a Frost
   relic. The old harvest/catch milestone unlocks were REMOVED — a second,
   invisible lock duplicating the ceiling.

CRAFTING: THE BLACKSMITH
================================================================================
 - STARTER BLACKSMITH: the first smithy offers two wooden tools drawn once per
   save (`scene_shops.js` `STARTER_SMITH_SLOTS`), paid in wood; once both are owned it
   becomes a normal forge.
 - BLACKSMITH FORGE: gear is forged from materials (`Gear.blacksmithRecipe`):
   tools, weapons and armour from the tier-matched bar (Wood at the bottom),
   the staff from gems plus a bar. Chest-only weapons are never forged. The
   smithy offers the lowest rung it can and leans to its own rank
   (`gear.js` `SMITHY_NEXT_RUNG_BIAS`, `SMITHY_OWN_TIER_BIAS`).
 - SMELTING (`Gear.smeltingRecipe`): the top bars are smelted from a magical
   flower plus the bar below; the lower bars come from mining. A stepper
   batches the smelt.

================================================================================
CONSUMABLES
================================================================================
 - Self-targeted consumables (potions, powders, the sapphire portal)
   are used via a USE button below the inventory bar while one is selected —
   the same spot as Eat. Tapping your own cell never uses one. Timed effects
   are rows of `Buffs.KINDS`.
 - POTION OF TAMING (`taming_potion`): lures nearby farm animals toward you and
   is consumed.
 - BOOK: read on pickup (it never sits in the bag). Each Book is the next page
   of `PLAY_TIPS` in curriculum order (`save.tipsRead`); after the course, a
   random tip or a bearing to the nearest unopened chest.
 - CAMPFIRE: a coal lights a fire on an empty bare cell. A fire lights, rests
   and wards on one ring (`FIRE_REST_R`), so it doubles as a perimeter ward in
   the wild; underground it wards only the shallow levels' monsters
   (`FIRE_WARD_MAX_DEPTH`). It persists (`save.fires`) until tapped out.

INVENTORY

 - an effectively infinite list shown a page at a time with ◀ ▶ paging.

 - per-item STACK CAP rises with the Bag relic (`stackCapForBags`); excess is

 - ITEMS ALWAYS RENDER AS THEIR GAME-ART SPRITE on every surface. Emoji is

ITEMS & RARITY
================================================================================
 - every item has a base tier 1..7 (`items.js` `BASE_TIER`) feeding one rarity
   picker (`rarity.js`) used by chests, treasure and fishing; numbers in
   `RARITY_TUNING`. Player-facing names are the rarity badges, Basic to Godly

 - the catalogue is the `ITEMS` table in `items.js` (prices in `PRICES`;

================================================================================
SHINY VARIANTS & DISCOVERY
================================================================================
 - a small fraction of flora, trees, animals, monsters, fish and rocks spawn as
   a "shiny" variant (`util.js` `SHINY_RATE`). Shiny status is a deterministic
   hash of the stable id — it survives reloads and rebuilds without storing
   anything — and the sprite sparkles.
 - harvesting / catching a shiny pays a headline coin bonus with a fanfare
   (`app.js` `awardShinyBonus`).
 - MEMORIES: the FIRST time you find a new kind of thing — a shiny of a kind,
   an elite of a kind — it is banked in `save.discovered` and a memory comes
   back (`app.js` `_bankDiscovery`) with a full energy bar. Memories are a
   save counter shown on the HUD's star chip, spent only at the WIZARD TOWER,
   and they drive the story (CLAUDE.md).

================================================================================
UNDERGROUND CAVES (Phase 1)
================================================================================
 - cave-rock clusters seed a descending STAIRCASE. Tapping one changes DEPTH
   (0 = surface). Depth is persisted.
 - each underground floor is the "NEGATIVE" of the floor above: walkable cells become
   cave floor; water, roads and buildings become solid rock. This recurses, so
   depth N derives deterministically from N-1 (and never from player edits —
   CLAUDE.md).
 - staircases mirror at the SAME world point: a level's down-stair is the next
   level's up-stair, with a fresh down-stair a few cells off.
 - tiles are cached PER DEPTH. Caves hold mineral rocks and hostile MONSTERS
   (no surface fauna), each an ENEMY fought on HP and paid with the bounty.
   Kinds are gated by depth windows so each level down reads as a new
   population (`enemy_roster.js` rows — `Combat.MONSTERS` is their view;
   giants and other variants are their own rows). Ranged foes need a CLEAR LINE, like your own bow.
 - monsters can't be tamed; a defeated one is recorded in `save.caught` so it
   stays dead across reloads.

================================================================================
SAVED GAMES (slots)
================================================================================
 - multiple named save SLOTS (`save.js`): a registry (`SAVES_KEY`) tracks the
   slots and the active one; each slot owns its own data key.
 - switching slots re-points `SAVE_KEY` and re-inits the scene and caches.
   Slots can be created, renamed and reset; listed newest-played first.

================================================================================
UI COLOUR LANGUAGE
================================================================================
Two colours carry MEANING; everything else is neutral chrome. A player glancing
at any screen should be able to answer "is this something I press, or something
I just won?" without reading a word.

 - BLUE-WHITE = TREASURE & POWERUPS — what the WORLD gives the player. Worn
   by the POI pads, the POI light, the reward ceremony modal, and powerup
   grants (gear equipped, Inner Light).

 - GOLD / YELLOW = PLAYER CONTROLS — what the PLAYER drives. Worn by buttons,
   the movement stick, inventory slots and tabs, HUD chrome and dialog frames,
   and coins (the currency the player spends sits on the control side).

 - EXCEPTIONS, and they are deliberate:
     * ITEM RARITY colours still colour a reward's NAME — rarity is information
       the blue-white frame doesn't carry.
     * ENERGY keeps green → gold → red as it drains; DANGER keeps red. The bar
       reports and cannot be pressed; it was briefly moved to amber to obey
       the law strictly and moved back — the traffic-light reading is an idiom
       players already know.
     * Ceremonies that are NOT loot reuse the reward modal with their own
       accent (passing out = orange, restoring a wreck = green) so a
       construction never reads as treasure.

 - ONE source of truth: the `UI_*` constants in `src/util.js` (canvas and
   Phaser), mirrored as CSS custom properties in `index.html` (DOM).
   `UI_CONTROL` names the control role, `UI_TREASURE*` the treasure role. Reach
   for the ROLE that fits — never add a near-duplicate shade, and change both
   halves together.

================================================================================
DIALOGS
================================================================================
 - EVERY dialog opens with a KIND HEADER: a hero icon and a one-word category
   over a hairline rule (a castle says QUEST, a chest TREASURE, a smithy FORGE).
   Dialogs used to open straight into flavour copy, which reads fine once you
   know where you are and not at all when you don't.
 - The label is the CATEGORY; the flavour line carries the specific offer. A
   caller may override the label for a one-off outcome and keep the icon.
 - `MODAL_KINDS` (`src/app.js`) is the whole taxonomy; shells are built by
   `makeModalShell` (CLAUDE.md owns the painting rules). A dialog about a thing
   on the map passes that thing's sprite; the category glyph is the fallback.
 - dialogs don't repeat their own name in a title line; the header says it
   once.
 - `showOfferModal` renders a TRADE — `get` is what you receive, `cost` what
   you pay, joined by "for". `cost` is optional; never fill both halves with
   the same text.
 - `tools/modal_audit.js` pins all of the above.

FOG OF WAR
 - Land the player has NEVER WALKED is washed near-black; walking reveals a
   disc around the player (`Fog.REVEAL_CELLS`), and what is revealed stays
   revealed, per save, forever.
 - The disc has a HARD CEILING below the viewport half-width: reveal that far
   and every cell is revealed the instant it is visible, so the fog would be
   stored and never once seen. `fog.test.js` pins the bound. (Row/column sweep
   arms were tried and removed: they cleared ground the player never went
   near. If the screen edge reads wrong, fix the RAMP, never widen the
   reveal.)
 - The wash is PURE BLACK, not the biome-tinted out-of-reach dim: that says
   "this biome, unlit"; fog says "you don't know". It stacks with the
   distance falloff — retune the pair together.
 - The wash is A CONTINUOUS FIELD in a canvas texture, darkening with distance
   from explored ground (`render.js` `FOG_ALPHA`), with the frontier bent by
   noise keyed on WORLD position so it reads as weather and the same tongue of
   fog sits over the same ground every time. The noise tapers to nothing at
   both ends, so walked ground stays clear and the deep interior stays fully
   dark. If you are tempted to draw the fog as rects again — at any resolution
   — that is the bug.
 - LAYER: the fog is above every world layer, labels included — a label
   poking through would name a shop the player has never found. The player's
   own kit stays above it. Surface only.
 - STORAGE is one BIT per cell, packed per tile and run-coded (`src/fog.js`,
   `save.fog`) — a Set of cell keys is right for dozens of cells, wrong for
   thousands. The masks live in fog.js, NOT on the tile cache, which is
   derived state and would wipe the history on prune or re-rasterise.
 - COST: the reveal and the rebuild happen on cell crossings; between them
   the fog layer scrolls. The repaint skips squares whose corners agree, so
   the work tracks the length of the frontier, not the area of the screen.
 - The STARTER TRAIL is revealed when it is laid (`fog.js`
   `_revealStarterTrail`), and this is not optional: the trail is a SIGHTLINE
   CHAIN, and without it every crate sat under the wash on a fresh save.
   Deliberately not one blanket radius round the anchor — that would reveal map
   the trail does not go to. `starter_relic.test.js` drives the real lift.
 - Fog DEFERS to the UNMAPPED VEIL: a cell whose tile hasn't arrived keeps the
   loading animation and becomes ordinary fog when the tile lands.
 - `tools/layer_audit.js` pins the layer and the texture it paints.

TEXTURES
 - EVERY opened chest VANISHES, crates included — there is no "looted" sprite.
   An emptied crate read as broken art and is worth less on the map than the
   clear cell under it. The tap target, pad and label are unaffected.
 - A texture key nothing creates fails SILENTLY (Phaser substitutes a
   checkerboard). `tools/shell_audit.js` checks both directions: every literal
   key `RENDER_SPEC` names is provided, and every texture maker is reachable at
   boot.

HUD
================================================================================
HOW THE COLOUR LANGUAGE LANDS ON THE HUD (see §UI COLOUR LANGUAGE above)
 - Every persistent CONTROL carries the control colour. At REST it wears a dim
   rim (the control colour with the light off, not a new hue); SELECTED lights
   to full gold with a glow. The resting rim must leave a clear step to the
   selected state, or an unselected slot reads as lit.
 - Surfaces that only REPORT give the colour up and wear neutral INK: a bright
   ink for what you read, a dusty one for what you glance at. The item name
   used to be gold — the loudest "reach for me" colour on the one thing you
   cannot touch.
 - The coin chip keeps gold, per §UI COLOUR LANGUAGE.
 - The world palette obeys the same law from the other side: no terrain colour
   is allowed into the yellow band (`COLORS` in `src/app.js`).
 - Statuses, buffs and timers are ONE status row (CLAUDE.md "Dialogs,
   feedback and teaching").

CHROME — salvaged field kit
 - One `--chrome-*` token vocabulary in `index.html` paints every persistent HUD
   surface as parts of one machine: SALVAGE — warm weathered steel, dusty lit
   edges, a faint scuff. Controls are RAISED; anything that HOLDS something is
   SUNK. The tokens are paint only, so adding chrome cannot move an element.

VERTICAL BUDGET (fitGame -> layOutVertically, index.html)
 - Three bands share the screen height and only the middle one is the game:
   the top chrome, the map, and the inventory cluster.
 - Slack is measured and spent in priority order: the walking stick's room
   first, then a breathing margin under the objective chip. (The game box was
   once pinned to the top with the HUD at the bottom, so all the slack landed
   in one unmeasured place and the stick sat on the map on small phones.)
 - ON A PHONE THE WIDTH WINS: a portrait viewport narrower than the column cap
   always fills edge to edge; only wider viewports get gutters. Capping the
   scale by the leftover band as well put black bars down both sides of
   ordinary iOS Safari sessions whenever the toolbars showed.
 - What gives instead is the VERTICAL budget: the map slides up under the
   objective chip (never over the coin/energy row), then at most one ring of
   cells tucks under the inventory; only a viewport too short for that shrinks
   the column.
 - The stick sits in the gap between map and tabs when it fits, otherwise
   over the map's bottom corner — never the inventory, never the map's centre.
 - `tools/layout_audit.js` pins these invariants across real device sizes,
   including the toolbars-showing case.

CLUTTER
 - The "no GPS — use the stick or WASD to move" line is a lesson, not a status:
   it retires the moment the player first steers by hand. "waiting for GPS…"
   is live status and stays until a fix lands.
 - The pager is only built when a category spans more than one page.
 - The developer readout sits behind ☰ › Developer. Not styled for production.

================================================================================
ARCHITECTURE: STANDARDIZED MODULES
================================================================================
The source has been progressively factored out of the monolithic app.js so the
common, repeated logic lives in one declarative or pure place. This is design
intent, not just cleanup — adding new content should mean editing data/cores,
not threading another branch through the dispatcher.

 - INTERACTABLE REGISTRY (`interactables.js` `INTERACTABLES`): every
   tap-driven WORLD OBJECT is one declarative row that separates its concerns
   — the tool that drives the wheel, whether it is spent, a pure gate that
   may refuse the tap, the up-front energy (also the cancel refund), and the
   payout (or a custom flow). One driver (`runInteractable`) walks them.
   Adding a new interactable = adding a row.
 - LUCK applies to chest/treasure loot only, read off `save` by `pickReward`
   (`rarity.js`): TIER luck (`upgradeLuck`) and QUANTITY luck (`qtyLuck`),
   both wizard rungs. Gather drops are not luck-aware; a declarative gather-luck
   field that never shipped on was removed rather than left as dead weight.
 - PURE CORES are extracted for headless testing (no scene, no DOM); the
   scene keeps thin wrappers that own only side effects: `inventory.js`
   (stack rules), `energy.js` (max / spend / offline rest), `crops.js`
   (growth, watering and raider rules — the single owner of
   `STAGE_HOLD_MS`), `home.js` (`HomeArea`: the canonical "near spawn?" test,
   loaded before worldgen). The kind tables CLAUDE.md lists ("Shared design
   rules") follow the same principle.
