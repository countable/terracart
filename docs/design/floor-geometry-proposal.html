Artistic setting and player experience

Draft for editing - what each floor should feel like and what it rewards. Mechanics live in the tables above; these notes own mood, tension and incentives.

Surface - the Mending Lane town

Setting. The Breaking's aftermath: overgrown streets, roofless houses, restoration scaffolds. Soft daylight; lamps returning street by street as the town heals.

Experience. The peaceful baseline. Home's ring is safe, risk grows with distance, and combat stays optional. The pull is restoration - delivery wishlists, build cards, neighbours returning - with light foraging and shopping between.

Floor 1 - natural caves

Setting. Wet stone and dripping water; sconce light and the first torch in your hand. The mirror tunnels each have a mood - roots, seep, miners' marks, bone galleries.

Experience. The friendly dark: the only floor where campfires keep monsters off, and a torch waits at the foot of every ladder. Spring caves and mushroom rings are the calm; bone galleries are the first real fright. Players learn light, first ore, and that pits are shortcuts with consequences.

Floor 2 - goblin city

Setting. A lamplit town under rock: carved facades, doors in the walls, stolen goods stacked in the warrens. The streets are open to the stone sky.

Experience. A hostile civic maze with a rhythm of risk: streets are the watched arteries, warrens are the reward. Route choice is the play - dash the open road or clear the rooms for their caches. The elevator stop is the strategic prize; taking it holds the gate to everything below.

Floor 3 - deep stone

Setting. Black stone, dwarven lamps, pale groves glowing in the clearings, old worked faces where someone mined before you.

Experience. Effort is the currency: every metre beyond a clearing is paid for in energy, so there are no casual dashes - preparation, food and a marked way out. Clearings are the rest stops, with residents, shrines and trade. Players who read the town above move smarter; ghosts keep the dark honest.

Floor 4 - fungal Underdark

Setting. One vast cavern under fungal light: blue-green glow, drifting spores, canopies you can walk beneath. The fungi are the torches; it is bright, by cave standards.

Experience. Visibility cuts both ways - you see far, and are seen. The floor invites the long walk: the portal stone waits a kilometre out in a good chest, so exploration is the quest itself. Fungal beds are the calm forage; crypt pockets and vents spike the middle.

Floor 5 - the haunted dead city

Setting. Your own city, repeated in ruin and silence: cracked pavement, gravestones where the shops stood, cold ghost-light in familiar windows.

Experience. Earned, not stumbled into - the trials hand it to you, and it plays on memory: you know these streets from above, which is an advantage and an unease at once. The haunt never relents, so the pattern is controlled clears and treasure search, not wandering. Gravestone caches and ghost bounties pay for the nerve.

Floor 6 - the lava stratum

Setting. Black rock and moving lava under the old footprints; ember light, heat shimmer, no place for a camp.

Experience. The floor is the enemy: heat taxes every stop and fires refuse to light, so runs are short and purposeful. Cool crystals become the rest stops players fight over. Infernals pay well - the efficient-run floor for geared players.

Floor 7 - the dragon hunting grounds

Setting. Ash drifts and old bones; scale-glint in the dark. The wizard's hunters walk here too - this is where his lessons were meant to land.

Experience. Apex-predator country: patrols punish wandering, and peace is what you carve, not what you find. The intended loop is hunt-craft: gear at the tower, track, engage, retreat. Dragon-kin bounties and the rungs toward floor 8 are the pull.

Floor 8 - the roosts

Setting. Vast lairs lit by hoard-glow: egg clutches, heaped treasure, a perched silhouette visible from across the cavern.

Experience. The heist floor. The hoard is in sight, guarded, and stealable - greed against the sleeping thing, with an escape route you planned on the way in. The game's highest stakes: T7 hoards, deep ore, and the dragon story's stage.
