Floor notes

Floor 1 - natural caves (decided; nothing to confirm)
Recognizes terrain paint (negative mirror), roads (street mirror + gate), nexus anchors (spring / goblin warren / mushroom / gemstone areas at groves, mine tunnels at quarries), POIs (chest mirrors, torch seats), transportation lines (themed routes), the spawn gate, and above-floor objects (holes fall to floor 2, quarry seams stamp down). Everything shipped.
Floor 2 - goblin city (P1-P4)
Buildings are the only walls; roads stay open. Warren rooms exist today as anchor-local nexus areas (goblin weight 45 at depth 2, budget 16); P1 extends them to full-floor coverage with the street mirror as the corridor net. Theme and foes decided (floors.md): the warren family, city window 2-3.
Floor 3 - deep stone (P6-P8)
The clearings mode ships today at depth 2 and shifts to 3 with the rework: park / grove / playground / pitch / commercial ground opens and seeds Deep groves and Dwarven cities. Digging is the way around (P6); about half the roads clear as tunnels (P7); the down shaft to floor 4 is dug open at a soft-stratum seam (P8).
Floor 4 - fungal Underdark (P9-P11)
Open mode (shipped at depth 3 today, shifts to 4). The portal stone waits here (T4+ chest a kilometre out), chest tiers first reach T6, crypt ghosts appear. Fungal beds follow the surface woods (P9), spore vents (P10), mushroom monsters join at window 4-5 (P11).
Floor 5 - haunted dead city (P12-P15)
The surface-mirror mode (shipped at depth 4 today, shifts to 5): the city above repeated in ruin. Entry through the arena hatches only (P14 seats them); gravestone caches and cracked pavement (P13); ruins stay solid-walled for now (P12); the haunt is constant (P15).
Floor 6 - lava stratum (P16-P17)
The above mirror plus lava under surface buildings (shipped at depth 5 today, shifts to 6). Infernals enter, fire elementals appear only here. Water stays wall by default (P16); heat drains unless you stand near a cool crystal (P17).
Floors 7-8 - dragon layer (P18-P19)
Above-mirror recursion (the default profile row). First T7 chests and ghost growth at 7; ore reaches T7 at 8. The wizard-tower key gates 6 to 7. One roost per habitat region on 8 (P18); 7 reads as hunting grounds (P19).
