Game terminology glossary

Markdown source

Purpose

Use one vocabulary for game design, comments and documentation. This glossary separates naming ambiguities, valid subconcepts and redundant synonyms. Inventory categories also define shared code keys; naming changes preserve mechanics.

Scope

This covers shared world-generation, layout, progression and interaction concepts in maintained source and design docs. It is not an exhaustive item, creature or story-character catalogue. Evidence links show representative definitions and uses.

These terms are approved for design prose, comments and documentation. The owning source and docs continue to define behavior. The inventory category policy below applies to code keys as well as prose. Other runtime identifiers and data keys retain their existing meanings.

  • Ambiguous use: a name has competing meanings; the column defines the terms for each meaning.
  • Subconcepts: valid narrower concepts or components to keep. Related concepts at the same level are identified as such rather than treated as synonyms.
  • Synonyms: wording to replace in design prose only when it means the same concept. This does not remove story language, map tags, API names or data keys.
  • Technical names: backticked identifiers remain intact unless the inventory category policy explicitly renames them.

Grouped rows keep related concepts together within each table. Title case is appropriate for category headings; lowercase common nouns in prose are fine. Preserve proper names such as Home and Dragon Hood.

Inventory categories and treasure rewards

Inventory categories use the same names in the UI, code and design prose. INV_CATS in items.js owns their labels and lowercase keys:

Inventory label Code category key Membership rule
Seeds seeds Items with kind seed.
Produce produce Items with kind produce, except explicit category overrides; also the fallback for unmapped kinds, including animal.
Pets pets Items with kind pet_accessory, plus owned pets from the pet lifecycle.
Relics relics Relic equipment and items with kind unique_relic.
Armour armour Armour equipment.
Ores ores Items with kind ores, including wood, metals and gems; Rock (rubble) joins through its category override.
Magic magic Items with kind magic.
Supplies supplies Items with kind supply.

Use Ores / ores for the former Minerals / mineral category, item kind and generic treasure reward class. Other item kinds, such as seed, supply, animal and unique_relic, and equipment types relic and armor describe behavior rather than inventory categories, so they retain their names. invCatForItem(id) maps an item to its official category; Rock keeps its produce kind for crop behavior and its ores category for inventory display.

Treasure rewards expose their official inventory category through reward.category, derived by rewardCategory(reward). The reward's cls selects its roll or handling path and need not equal its inventory category. Coins are currency, so coin rewards have no inventory category.

Reward pools are named selection subgroups, owned by ChestThemes.groups in chest_themes.js. They keep their own membership, weights and fallbacks. materials and gems are separate pools whose items appear under Ores; naming the category Ores does not add gems to a materials roll. books is a Supplies subgroup. A pool can cross inventory categories: medicalMagic includes Magic items and healing amulets under Relics. The category alignment preserves existing pool membership and reward odds. See themed chests for selection rules.

Cleanup boundaries

  1. Use Nexus for the whole place, Nexus focal layout for its central arrangement, and Temple-bearing Nexus for the subset permitted to claim a temple.
  2. Use Major, Medium, Small and Path for the four road tiers. Name the Major-and-Medium road group and Variant-eligible Small roads explicitly; preserve their safety and variant memberships.
  3. Replace synonyms only in the meanings stated in each row. Keep source-map tags, proper names and valid subconcepts. Runtime identifiers follow the explicit inventory category policy; other identifiers retain their names.
  4. Keep this glossary about shared vocabulary. Detailed mechanics, numeric tuning, item/creature catalogues and story canon stay in their owning sources.
Live tools use current game definitions. Saved reviews are snapshots; their original findings and artwork are preserved.
