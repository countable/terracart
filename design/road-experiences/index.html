Road experiences

What each place should make the player feel, and how its art and interactions can deliver that experience.

Original story art, current game previews, and three adjectives define the intended feel. Use Comment to suggest changes.

01

The hedged lane

Road label: streetname Hedged

In-game feel

Orderly · Reassuring · Watchful

Feeling on entry

A carefully kept lane feels reassuring until something moves behind the hedge.

Straight hedge edges and white lamps frame clean paving, while rounded tree crowns hide the ground beyond.

In-game art direction

  • Keep the regular formal setts, clipped hedge row and white lamps just off the road.
  • Use rounded crowns on detected trees within three cells; keep the ground free of grass tufts.
  • Let the existing hedge art conceal the ambush approach without obscuring the road.

Player actions

  • In gameTwo goblin runts hide in the clipped hedge. One ambush is attempted per hedged street per map tile, where a valid spawn spot exists.
02

Gone to seed

Road label: streetname Overgrown

In-game feel

Reflective · Wild · Weathered

Feeling on entry

Walking the road feels like moving through the life of a forest.

Tiny seedlings give way to young pines, mature crowns, bare trunks and stumps, with a shrine beyond the last group.

In-game art direction

  • Keep one verge row with one empty cell between specimens and clearly distinct growth stages.
  • Use the existing pine, dead-tree and stump art; omit shrubs and extra tree rows.
  • Keep the ground free of grass and shrubs so the tree sequence and final shrine remain readable.
  • Leave scattered water puddles on the verge opposite the tree sequence.

Player actions

  • In gameThe generated row follows the growth-to-stump sequence and seats its shrine after the stumps.
  • In gamePlant guards and a wild-boar encounter interrupt the woodland walk; some streets also have a bear.

Orchard Lane

Road label: streetname Orchard

In-game feel

Inviting · Fruitful · Vulnerable

Feeling on entry

Food within reach makes the lane inviting, but harvesting asks the player to lower their guard.

Fruit catches the eye among rounded crowns immediately beside the road, with a farmer goblin farther along the row.

In-game art direction

  • Keep fruit trees and mature maples in the first verge row, with blocked seats left empty.
  • Make apples readable through foliage; reserve orange and Worldpeach colors for occasional trees.
  • Keep the current soft pink lamp glow restrained so it does not compete with the fruit colors.

Player actions

  • In gameThe lane supplies fruit trees, mostly apples with occasional oranges and Worldpeaches.
  • In gameFarmer-goblin guards make some harvesting stops contested.

Quiet · Deliberate · Contemplative

Feeling on entry

A quiet passage becomes a short, deliberate route-planning puzzle.
