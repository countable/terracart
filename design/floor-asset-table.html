Underground Level Table

One row per level of the new ladder. Each level re-skins the surface's buildings and roads, dresses its landcover, gives the shared new mechanics its own art, and adds objects of its own. Every tile is 1×1 or 1×2, except the goblin tents and carpets, which are flat floor decals, cut at 48 px, resized to 24 and shown at the cell size below.

Proposed import plan — not yet applied to the game. Bring in environmental art that needs no new gameplay mechanics. Preserve existing collision, routes, hazards, harvesting and lighting. Decorative props have no interaction prompts or rewards.

Build order: quiet ground and wall materials → road surfaces and edges → sparse static dressings. Start with new L1 sewer, then L3–L7. The former L1 cave generation and gameplay move to L2; its approved Abandoned Mines walls now use the lighter earthy top tone. Applied wall schemes and placement rules appear in each asset row below. New L4 mechanics

Rune puzzle

Dwarven city floors carry rune-letter plates (the eight letters and three coloured plates in L4's New mechanics). A rune plaque by a vault door shows a three- or four-rune word. The player steps on the plates in that order; each step is judged per cell, and a wrong step resets the sequence, the same way the temple's path trial judges moves. Spelling the word opens the city's deep cache. The word and the plate layout derive from the tile and cell, so every player sees the same puzzle.

Carts

Ore carts sit on the cart rails that run along L4's stone roads, the mirrored surface streets. An empty cart fills with the ore of rocks mined beside it, and its look follows the ore: coal, copper, silver, gold or gems. Pushing a loaded cart rolls it along the rail to the end of the line, where it unloads into a stockpile by the forge. Mining stays the existing mineral-rock mechanic; the cart only moves the haul.

Wall lanterns

A shared slot next to wall torches: a wall-mounted light source with each level's own art. L1 and L3 borrow the dwarven iron lanterns because their packs have none.

Buildings and Roads open with a swatch of the level's footprint and road treatment: brick masses and sewer channels (L1), rock with solid stone roads (L2, L4, L7), rock with timber-kerbed underground roads (L3), fungal masses and slime rivers (L5), lava pits and ember paths (L6). Under each level name: the art tilesets its tiles come from, with tile counts; grounds without a set name come from the stock ground sheets. A dashed slot in New mechanics means the packs have no 1×1 or 1×2 tile for it on that level. Chests, rocks, pots, barrels, crates, coins and campfires reuse the game's existing art and are left out.
