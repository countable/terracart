Art direction

The new tiles use a limited four-tone cel-shaded palette, nearly flat base tiles and two relief variants, each with one or two larger connected details. Dips use a clean shadow crescent and short highlight lip; broad flat colour replaces scattered texture noise. Ordinary ground highlights and shadows stay within about ten RGB levels of the terrain colour; lava uses a brighter red, orange and gold palette. They take their pixel clusters from the reference and character, while retaining each terrain’s existing overall tone. The character stays the focal point. Sand retains its approved original procedural appearance.
