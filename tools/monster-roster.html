Rules, armour and how to read the numbers

Combat values use the current game’s combat helpers. Kill time counts a full weapon interval before the first hit and assumes every hit connects with one target, without class bonuses, pets, potions, regeneration or travel.

Group cost is an estimate: surviving enemies attack continuously while the player defeats them one at a time. It includes selected aura exposure and staff casting costs. Movement, missed attacks and piercing can change the result. Ghosts and traps are special encounters outside this sustained model.

Habitat filters show declared eligibility. Habitat families, zone guards, home protection and spawn placement can further constrain encounters.
