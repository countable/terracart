How to read this page

Sprites are drawn at their in-game size, magnified by the zoom setting, on meadow green. Current art uses the game's sheets and zone tints. Proposed art comes from unused_art/, which is git-ignored; it shows as missing on a machine without those packs.

Facings are shown in each sheet's own row order (rows 0 to 3). The packs disagree on which row faces left or right; confirm before wiring a sheet into SpriteLayout.

Current neighbours pick one of the citizen sheets at random and wear their zone's tint; a role with its own sheet, like Orrin, keeps it. Each proposal gives every role one sheet of its own, so no two roles look alike; a role shown in several zones, such as the Lamplighter, looks the same in each. Proposals and carries authored colours that would skip the tint. A Shared with tag marks a pick that another role or a reserved character already uses, and Looks different in marks one role drawn two ways; alternatives already taken say who uses them.
