How to read this page

Sprites are drawn at their in-game size, magnified by the zoom setting, on meadow green. A role with its own sheet shows it once; a role that still uses the shared citizen sheets shows each of them in its culture's tints.

A label used in several cultures (Peddler, Lamplighter) is one role with one look, so it has one row listing each culture and its share there.
