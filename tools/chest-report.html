City chest expectations

Each city covers nine surface tiles. Counts exclude shops, service buildings, pots, barrels and courier posts from ordinary chest rewards. The nearby count uses a 400 m circle around Kelowna’s Home or each other city’s review centre. This is a base-tile POI report; live Overpass additions and later gameplay placements are outside its scope.

Expected rewards per opening

2,000 seeded rolls per theme and tier, using the current picker and a fresh player with no upgrades. Percentages estimate the chance of receiving an item; quantities and coins are means per opening. These are simulations, not exact probability tables.
