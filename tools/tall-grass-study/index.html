DRAGON HOOD / INTERACTIVE MATERIAL STUDY

A field you disappear into.

One square of dense, waist-high grass. Walk through it; tap to gather it and reveal what is underneath.

Walk through the cover

Use WASD, arrow keys or the buttons. Tap a covered square to clear it. A little chest sits under the centre cell; the tall post stays visible. Enter a square hiding a find for an “oh!” pop. Eggs occur in 1% of grass cells, without a wiggle.

Visual prototype · 32-unit cells, half-depth ground projection, screen-vertical height. Both covers are walkable, with no jump. Picking grass adds the existing longgrass item; hedge clearing is preview-only. Scene and inventory reset on reload.
