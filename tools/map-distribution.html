How these figures are built

The selected city's 3×3 tile block (nine tiles), rasterized live in this page with the shipping WorldGen.rasterizeTile and the scene's own spawnInTile, exactly as map review builds them. Home is placed far off the block, so no starting area is counted. Counts and reward values cover the whole area. Percentages describe its combined terrain or placements; recurring rewards are shown per day.
