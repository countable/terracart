What changed recently (world rules the figures below reflect)
  • Unclassified landuse is WASTELAND terrain (was painted as residential).
  • Road mask stamps a cell when the drawn band covers ≥ 50% of it.
  • Surface traps only on bandit stretches of major roads (~1/3 of each street's ~200 m stretches, ROAD_CLASS_BANDIT_VERGE) at 6× the ground share, + wasteland; per-tile totals ~1.5× the old whole-verge rule. Residential streets are safe.
  • Fauna attractors (one lane, attracts columns): dogs → major verges (all); deer → orchard lanes + groves, cats → Lantern Row, crows → Pilgrim's Way + churchyards, butterflies → overgrown / toadstool lanes + groves, slimes → wasteland, rabbits → hedgerow / overgrown (no wild rabbits spawn yet) — half of each species moves, none are added.
  • Only ~1 in 3 major-road bus stops is a broken wagon + goblin; the rest stay bus-stop chests.
  • Street variants per street size (minor: hedgerow, overgrown, orchard, pilgrim, toadstool 5%; major: lantern, burned, barricade). Every barricade holds a goblin; every burned-row stretch one fire slime. Lamps glow in their street's colour.
  • Rocks line ~25% of minor streets (never a hedgerow), now ~1 per 10 m (was 16 m); wasteland lots keep the rock-cluster scatter.
  • Tar and stakes slow the body.
  • Influence zones (grove / old stones / tar yard) repaint lot ground; each anchor's nexus chest pays tier +1; a nexus pattern is whole across tile seams. Tar-yard ground is untillable; fire slimes are off the quest board.
