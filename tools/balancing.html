What changed recently (world rules the figures below reflect)
  • Chest tier = density (Sep 2026): a POI chest's tier is how many chests of its own class its tile holds (CHEST_DENSITY_TIERS: 1 → T4, 2–4 → T3, 5–24 → T2, 25+ → T1), + depth, +1 at a nexus. The Home rings are gone — the tier shown is the tier paid. A T1 chest is a crate that restocks after clamp(floor(n / 25), 1, 7) days (25–49 of a kind daily … 175+ weekly; the day ledger now keeps a week). Public art is a fixed T1 one-time trunk. Bins / recycling are barrels: smashed, restock like crates, empty 60% → 90% at 25+ of a kind, else 1–3 coins / an apple / a torch or rope. ATMs alone are pots of gold, spilling 30 coins alone → 3 at 50 per tile → 1 at 150+ (log-linear). Bike racks lend +100% stick speed for 3 min once a day. Gates are two posts round a spawn point (one slime or goblin a UTC day, every mode). Information POIs are notice boards (one Book page each). Traffic signals, crossings, stop signs, fences, power lines and carports are no longer chests (Kelowna's sidecar: 55 removed; the MVT tiles carry none).

  • Unclassified landuse is WASTELAND terrain (was painted as residential).
  • Road mask stamps a cell when the drawn band covers ≥ 50% of it.
  • Surface traps only on bandit stretches of major roads (~1/3 of each street's ~200 m stretches, ROAD_CLASS_BANDIT_VERGE) at 6× the ground share, + wasteland; per-tile totals ~1.5× the old whole-verge rule. Residential streets are safe.
  • Fauna attractors (one lane, attracts columns): dogs → major verges (all); deer → orchard lanes + groves, cats → Lantern Row, crows → Pilgrim's Way + churchyards, butterflies → overgrown / toadstool lanes + groves, slimes → wasteland, rabbits → hedgerow / overgrown (no wild rabbits spawn yet) — half of each species moves, none are added.
  • Only ~1 in 3 major-road bus stops is a broken wagon + goblin; the rest stay bus-stop chests.
  • Street variants per street size (minor: hedgerow, overgrown, orchard, pilgrim, toadstool 5%; major: lantern, burned, barricade). Every barricade holds a goblin; every burned-row stretch one fire slime. Lamps glow in their street's colour.
  • Rocks line ~25% of minor streets (never a hedgerow), now ~1 per 10 m (was 16 m); wasteland lots keep the rock-cluster scatter.
  • Tar and stakes slow the body.
  • Influence zones (grove / old stones / tar yard) repaint lot ground; each anchor's nexus chest pays tier +1; a nexus pattern is whole across tile seams. Tar-yard ground is untillable; fire slimes are off the quest board.
  • Park characters: every park polygon is a meadow (long grass + wildflowers), wooded (trees, shrubs, mushrooms), formal (clipped hedge rows + marigolds) or common (open lawn) — by a hash of the named-park POI inside it, else its own centroid. The grove, its POI pad and its nexus read the same character.
  • Park density ~40% lower (Kelowna fixtures: 18.1% → 10.5% of park cells hold something), still in clumps with near-bare lawn between (FLORA_PATCH.sparse 0.3 → 0.15).
  • Park fringe: every park polygon (named or not) spills a ragged ≤ 20 m band of GROVE (CHURCHYARD round a cemetery) over lot ground, and a light smattering of its character's filler out to 30 m.
  • Churchyards: churches and cemeteries always get headstones — a per-cell rule on the grave-row lattice that wraps the building (no fixed pattern); every place of worship scatters plain rocks (~2.3× before), all one single-stone look. Churchyard ground is a mossier grey-green.
  • Grove figures: symmetric nexus aspects (compass roses ~1 park in 6–7, mirrored flower beds, diagonal trees / shrubs) laid whole or not at all; roses stay the rarer prize. A blocked pattern piece walks outward along its ray.
  • One barricade (+ goblin) and one waystone per street per tile (was one at every line end: Seattle 220 → 9 barricades, 164 → 28 waystones over 9 tiles).
  • A dog whose spawn draw was only blocked by a generated piece is seated on the bandit verge instead of lost.
  • Macro stalls: in-building POIs are no longer chests but places you come back to (looks macro_inn, macro_chapel, macro_apothecary, macro_scriptorium, macro_guildhall, macro_curio, macro_sundries, macro_training in "chests by look"; never opened, no gem, no pad). Inn (lodging) rests to full once a day at half a Vigor potion's coins per energy; chapel (place of worship) pays a daily roll one tier under the chest (+1 nexus in a churchyard); apothecary (pharmacy / dentist / hospital) sells one T2 potion + the antidote; scriptorium (library / college) sells Books + a torch (no free page); apothecary, sundries and scriptorium are plain stalls (ShopsMath.standPrice, the market stall's own counter); guildhall (town hall / police / fire station) posts one monster bounty a day — 1–3 surface foes (slime → goblin / archer by weapon tier) seated ~5 cells off by findWalkableDestination; each kill pays its enemyBounty coin and clearing pays the wages again (surface: $2 at tier 0–1, up to $21–30 at tier 6–7); curio hall (museum / theatre / cinema) pays no coin: one shared list of 22 lasting items, a memory at 5 / 10 / 15 given; sundries (generic shop) sells one supply item; training hall (sports centre / yoga) sells +1% damage for good (to +25%) or +10% for 24 h. A shop whose MVT subclass names produce (convenience, florist, coffee, confectionery…) is now a market stall first.
